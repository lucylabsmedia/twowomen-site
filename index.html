Our Mission

Meet Penny and Beth

We're two women of color, both over 60, connecting with other women in and around our age group.

TwoWomen is not out to change the world. Nope. Earth-shattering change is not our mission. Instead, at TwoWomen, we're all about helping make your current world richer, less stressful, more playful.

Through our blog we chat about every topic that's important to you; from the practical to the possible, the serious to the silly. And we don't shy away from conversations that might be a bit awkward, delicate, or difficult.
